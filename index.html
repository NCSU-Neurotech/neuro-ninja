Calibrate about 1 minute

Look at a dot in the middle for 20 seconds, then at each tile when it gets a red outline. After that, the games judge every tile against your own resting brain activity. That stops a tile from being picked when you aren't choosing anything, most of all a 10 Hz tile, where your alpha rhythm sits.

Accuracy test about 1 minute

A red outline shows where to look, and the game checks whether it picked that tile. Guessing gets 25%. Run it before playing to see how well your electrodes are working today.

How to play

In every game, four tiles flicker in the corners of the screen, each at its own rate (12, 13, 14 and 15 times a second, or 7.5 to 12 with the other rate set). Look straight at a tile and your visual cortex starts pulsing at its rate. The blue ring around the tile fills as the game gets sure, then it counts as a pick.

🍉 Fruit Slice
Fruit lands on a tile: look at it to slice it. Don't look at bombs. A dropped fruit or a sliced bomb costs a life, and three ends the game. Quick slices score 2, and 5 in a row adds +5.
🍊 Fruit Zen
The same, for 90 seconds, with no bombs and no lives.
🔨 Whack-a-Mole
Moles pop out of the holes: look at one to whack it before it ducks. Golden moles score 3. Whacking a bunny costs 5 seconds. 60 seconds, and the moles get quicker as the clock runs down.
🎵 Simon
The tiles light up one after another while they're still. Then they flicker and you repeat the sequence by looking at each in turn. One more step every round, three mistakes and it's over. The results show your pick accuracy.
🧀 Maze
The tiles are arrows. Look at one to send the mouse that way: it runs to the next fork on its own. Reach the cheese, and the next maze is bigger. 3 minutes.

Sit still and keep your jaw relaxed; blinking is fine. Esc or Space pauses. In the simulator, point the mouse at a tile instead of looking at it.

Uses the same decoder as EXG Visualizer+ (filter-bank CCA, 2 s windows). Close EXG Visualizer+ and any other program using the board before connecting: only one can hold the port.

⚠ Epilepsy warning

This game shows tiles that flicker 7.5 to 15 times per second. Flicker in this range can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy, and can cause headaches, eye strain or nausea in others.

  • Don't play if you have epilepsy or have ever had a seizure.
  • Stop right away if you feel unwell. Esc stops the flicker immediately.

Fisher et al. (2005), Epilepsia 46:1426. Not a medical device.
